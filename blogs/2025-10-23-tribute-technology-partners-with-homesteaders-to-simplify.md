Tribute Technology is partnering with Homesteaders Life Company, one of the most trusted names in funeral service, to make preneed policy management easier for funeral homes. With the soon-to-be-released integration, funeral directors will be able to view and connect Homesteaders policies directly within Tribute Management Software . Policy information will update automatically each night, so details will always be current.
