The new Benchmarking the Modern Obituary study from Tribute Technology reveals how small changes — like timing — can make a big difference for families and your business. When a death ocurs, people start searching online almost immediately, looking for service details, ways to share condolences, or simply to confirm the news. If the obituary isn’t posted yet, that moment of connection can pass you by.
