A simple way to expand your reach and bring more families to your website Tribute Technology has partnered with ObituaryShare to help funeral homes reach more families and communities online. With this new integration, funeral homes using Tribute websites can automatically publish obituaries to Facebook and local news websites with no extra steps or effort required. Each post includes the loved one’s name, photo, and service details, and links directly back to the funeral home’s Tribute website.
